## Product & Strategy Highlights

### Mission
- **"Democratizing finance for all"** — licensed securities path + permissionless on-chain path for non-sanctioned users with custodial/non-custodial wallets.

### Why the chain exists
- Own end-to-end stack; permissionless, open; bring liquidity and assets on-chain.
- Strategic pillar — **10-year horizon**, not a 6-month experiment (38:29).

### Hero products
1. **Stock tokens (RWA)** — security/debt-backed; primary market KYC-gated; secondary DEX trading permissionless (with compliance rules).
2. **Robinhood Wallet** — owned UX surface; partners with MetaMask and other wallets (co-marketing coming); not zero-sum vs third-party wallets.
3. **Robinhood Earn** — USDG lending via Stakehouse + **Morpho**; US-eligible states only.
4. **Perps** — partnership with **Hyperliquid** (ASR: "Leiter").

### Builder / ecosystem stance
- **No foundation, no token, no general grants** — support via **Open House** and merit-selected builders (22:16–23:19).
- Vision filter: **RWA + democratizing finance**; traction required (26:06).
- **Will not** mass-retweet or endorse projects — public-company liability + compliance (29:44–31:45).
- Permissionless chain: anyone can deploy (NFTs, DeFi, etc.); success metric = **fees generated by projects** (35:25).
- Retail-first B2C philosophy — not whale-only (36:39).

### Liquidity (7-day depth)
- **#1 operational challenge** — market hours vs after-hours vs weekends (15:08).
- Mint/burn bottleneck: underlying stock trades **weekdays only** → cash in/out of primary market (42:19–42:54).
- **Confidential 24/7 functionality** coming for onboarded LPs/issuers; improvement expected in **6–10 weeks** (28:23, 43:07).
- Primary market **not closed** to external LPs — dozens onboarded; <5 RH partner LPs (41:49–42:08).

### Compliance boundaries (Q&A)
- Cannot provide legal/compliance advice on stock-token-as-collateral DeFi (48:09).
- Points builders to **official stock-token docs + prospectus** per underlying (48:23).
- Aggressive/exotic yield strategies **not** for licensed brokerage app — better suited to non-custodial self-custody users (39:34–40:24).
